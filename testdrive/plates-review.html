Plates, by eye

Would you use this image as a surface, background or compositing element? Judge visual usefulness, visible watermarks, distracting subjects and near-duplicates. Open the source to verify resolution before marking usable. “Broken” means the preview failed; check the source before deciding it is unavailable.

This is a metadata pilot. Provider links may be resized images. Previews load directly from Flickr and Rawpixel; no image corpus is stored here.

Review and decision rules

Review all 10,000 candidates before applying the 50% rule. The lower bound counts usable labels only; the upper bound assumes every remaining candidate is usable. Unknown labels never count as rejected. Source totals expose supply differences; this first sample does not prove a 500k yield. Labels are saved in this browser when storage is available. Export a backup after each session and import it to resume in another browser.
